      How this workflow runs

      This page is the map, and the Command Center folders hold the documents. Keep the two in step.

      1. Spot a jobAdd an opportunity with a Target stage and a priority. Save the posting PDF in 04 High Priority Targets/<bucket>.
      2. Tailor & applyBuild the resume and cover letter from 01 Master Resume. Copy 02 Applications Submitted/03 Company Name - Copy This One, rename it, and file the posting, resume and letter in it.
      3. Log itSet the stage to Applied and enter the date applied. An "Applied" entry is added to the Activity Log for you, and a follow-up date appears.
      4. Follow upThe Dashboard lists everything due. When you follow up or hear back, use Log touch on the card. It updates Last Contact and resets the clock.
      5. InterviewsMove through Phone Screen → First → Final → Waiting Professionally. Log each conversation and send the thank-you.
      6. OffersMove to Offer – Negotiating and fill a column in Offer Compare. Save the paperwork in 05 Offers.
      7. Close outPick the matching Closed stage: Filled, Rejected, Withdrawn, Ghosted or Vanished. After the ghost window, the page will suggest closing it.
      8. Move the folderEach opportunity shows the Command Center folder it belongs in for its current stage.
      9. Friday reviewCheck this week's applications against your goal, your interview rate and anything overdue. Note lessons in 99 Archive - Search For Job/01 Lessons From The Adventure. Then click Backup.
